## 📘 Project Overview
This homework investigates the robotic control of a slender elastic beam modeled as a two-dimensional mass–spring chain. 
The beam is actuated through time-varying Dirichlet boundary conditions applied at its right end, which represent a planar robotic end-effector commanding the beam tip’s position and orientation.
The objective is to design and simulate a control strategy such that the middle node of the beam tracks a prescribed trajectory under gravity.
